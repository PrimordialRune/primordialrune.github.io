A LETTER

Hi, I’m Omar.

I design board games and video games, and I care most about the metaphor a game is built on: the object you hold, the medium it arrives in, the story it tells by how it plays.

I grew up on the Game Boy and the GBA, and Japanese games still shape most of what I make. Everything on the desk below is something I made. The first four open into a case study.
