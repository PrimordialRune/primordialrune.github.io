# Paragame / Metaphor

## Fun Facts

- Metagame exists in the space between the game and the player
- Breaking the fourth wall creates memorable gaming moments
- Games can comment on their own medium through mechanics
- The "magic circle" defines where reality ends and game begins
- Ludonarrative harmony: when mechanics reinforce story themes

## Random Facts Pool

- Undertale uses save files as a narrative device
- Fighting game metagames evolve independently from patch notes
- Speedrunning creates an entirely different game from the intended experience
- ARGs blur the line between game and reality
- Games about games: meta-commentary through interactive medium
