# Roleplay (RPG)

## Fun Facts

- Character progression creates emotional investment through mechanical growth
- The "hero's journey" structure maps perfectly to RPG pacing
- Turn-based combat prioritizes strategy over reflexes
- Party composition creates social dynamics even in single-player games
- Stats and numbers make abstract growth tangible and satisfying

## Random Facts Pool

- JRPGs and WRPGs evolved from the same roots into distinct philosophies
- The random encounter was born from hardware limitations
- Experience points gamify real-world concepts of growth and mastery
- Class systems create replayability through different playstyles
- The first RPGs were direct adaptations of tabletop rules
