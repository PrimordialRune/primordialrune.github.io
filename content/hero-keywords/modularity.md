# Modularity

## Fun Facts

- Modular game design enables endless replayability through recombination
- Procedural generation is modularity at the algorithmic level
- Deck-building games are modular by nature
- User-generated content extends game life through community modularity
- Component-based design makes games expandable and maintainable

## Random Facts Pool

- LEGO games embody physical modularity in interactive form
- Roguelikes use modular rooms and items for infinite variety
- Modding communities keep games alive decades after release
- Procedural music adapts to gameplay through modular composition
- Trading card games are modular economy simulators
