# Strategy

## Fun Facts

- Strategic depth emerges from simple rules with emergent complexity
- The best strategy games teach you by letting you fail
- Decision trees vs intuition: different player types approach strategy differently
- Perfect information vs hidden information creates vastly different strategic landscapes
- Tempo, resources, and positioning: the three pillars of strategy

- Card games blend strategy with probability management
- Strategy games train pattern recognition and forward planning
- The "fog of war" mechanic revolutionized real-time strategy
- Economic strategy: balancing short-term gains vs long-term investment
