# Asymmetry

## Fun Facts

- Asymmetric game design creates unique player experiences where each role feels distinct
- Games like Root and Vast pioneer asymmetric board gaming
- Asymmetry can exist in information, abilities, objectives, or starting conditions
- The challenge lies in balancing vastly different gameplay patterns
- Asymmetric PvP creates memorable "David vs Goliath" moments

## Random Facts Pool

- In Root, each faction has completely different victory conditions
- Asymmetry in fighting games: grapplers vs zoners create dynamic matchups
- The joy of mastering "your" character in asymmetric games
- Information asymmetry: hidden traitor mechanics create social tension
- Economic asymmetry: different resource engines require different strategies
