# Tactical Card Game - Design Document

## Game Overview

A 2-4 player tactical card game where players build decks and deploy units on a hex-based battlefield. Victory comes through strategic positioning, resource management, and clever use of card synergies.

## Core Systems

### Deck Building
- 40-card minimum deck size
- 3 faction system with neutral cards
- Card rarity tiers (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary)
- Deckbuilding restrictions to encourage variety

### Resource System
- Energy-based economy
- +1 max energy per turn (up to 10)
- Card costs ranging from 0-10 energy
- Resource denial mechanics available but limited

### Combat Resolution
- Turn-based activation
- Unit stats: Attack, Defense, Movement, Range
- Rock-paper-scissors damage types
- Status effects and buffs/debuffs

## Game Flow

### Setup Phase
1. Shuffle and draw starting hand (5 cards)
2. Place starting units on deployment zones
3. Determine first player

### Turn Structure
1. Upkeep (draw card, gain energy)
2. Main Phase (play cards, move units, activate abilities)
3. Combat Phase (declare attacks, resolve damage)
4. End Phase (discard to hand limit, end-of-turn effects)

## Design Pillars

### 1. Meaningful Decisions
Every turn should present interesting choices:
- Which cards to play vs hold
- Where to position units
- When to attack vs defend

### 2. Interaction
Multiple ways to interact with opponent:
- Direct combat
- Board control
- Resource disruption
- Hand disruption (limited)

### 3. Skill Expression
High skill ceiling through:
- Prediction and bluffing
- Resource optimization
- Positioning and zone control
- Deck composition

## Faction Design

### Faction A: Aggro Rush
- Low-cost units
- High mobility
- Burst damage
- Fragile but explosive

### Faction B: Control
- Removal spells
- Defensive units
- Card draw
- Late-game power

### Faction C: Midrange
- Balanced stats
- Versatile options
- Value generation
- Adaptable strategy

## Balance Philosophy

- Rock-paper-scissors faction matchups (45-55% win rates)
- Sideboarding options for competitive play
- Regular balance patches based on play data
- No pay-to-win mechanics

## Playtesting Insights

After 50+ playtest sessions:
- Average game length: 25-35 minutes
- First player advantage: ~52% (acceptable)
- Most engaging board states: 8-12 units total
- Players enjoy combo potential within factions

## Production Considerations

### Physical Version
- 300 unique cards across 3 factions
- Premium card stock with linen finish
- Modular hex tiles for battlefield
- Custom dice for tracking
