## Overview

A VR escape room prototype exploring natural interaction design and physics-based puzzles using Unity's XR Interaction Toolkit.

## Design Philosophy

### Presence First
- Natural hand interactions
- No UI overlays
- Environmental storytelling
- Diegetic feedback

### Intuitive Puzzles
- Real-world logic applies
- Visual affordances guide players
- Progressive complexity
- Multiple solution hints

## Interaction Design

### Hand Tracking
- Grab and release objects
- Two-handed manipulation
- Fine motor interactions
- Gesture recognition

### Physics System
- Realistic object weights
- Collision-based puzzles
- Stacking and balancing
- Chain reactions

## Puzzle Types

### Mechanical
- Locks and keys
- Gear mechanisms
- Pressure plates
- Levers and switches

### Logic
- Color matching
- Symbol sequences
- Mathematical clues
- Environmental codes

## Technical Challenges

- Hand occlusion handling
- Comfortable locomotion
- Physics stability in VR
- Performance optimization

## Learnings

VR design requires rethinking traditional game design conventions. What works on a screen often fails in VR, and vice versa.
