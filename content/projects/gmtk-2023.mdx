## Overview

Roles Reversed flips the typical RPG narrative by putting players in control of the final boss. Defend your dungeon against waves of incoming heroes using traps, minions, and boss abilities.

## Game Design

### Theme Interpretation
The GMTK 2023 theme "Roles Reversed" inspired us to explore the villain's perspective, giving players empathy for the other side of the typical hero's journey.

### Core Mechanics
- Place traps and minions between waves
- Manage limited resources
- Use boss abilities at critical moments
- Survive all hero waves to win

### Hero Types
- Warriors (high HP, slow)
- Rogues (fast, dodge traps)
- Mages (ranged attacks)
- Healers (support other heroes)

## Development

### Godot Experience
First project using Godot 4.0, leveraging:
- GDScript for rapid prototyping
- Built-in animation tools
- Node-based scene system

### 48-Hour Timeline
- Hour 0-8: Core mechanics
- Hour 8-24: Content creation
- Hour 24-40: Polish and balance
- Hour 40-48: Bug fixes and submission

## Results

Placed in top 15% for creativity and gameplay. The role reversal resonated with players who enjoyed the fresh perspective on classic RPG tropes.
